How we measure our advertising

Nano Motion uses the ChatGPT Ads Measurement Pixel (provided by OpenAI) to understand whether our ads in ChatGPT lead to visits, purchases and Club sign-ups. This page explains what is sent and how to control it. (This is a demo store built for a measurement exercise.)

What stays in your browser

Your bag, market and consent choice are kept in your browser's local storage. If you have agreed to measurement, the SHA-256 hashes of identifiers you entered are kept there too, so later pages can pass them to the pixel. Raw email, phone and names are never stored. Declining removes the stored hashes along with the pixel's cookies.

Your choices

  • United Kingdom and Germany: nothing is sent until you accept the measurement banner. Events from before you accept are not sent later. Declining removes the pixel's cookies.
  • United States and Japan: measurement is on by default. Use "Your privacy choices" in the footer to opt out at any time.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out in every market and send nothing.

Specific legal requirements per country are set with our privacy counsel. This page demonstrates the mechanism.

Measurement data is used to report on and improve advertising performance. OpenAI states that data collected through the pixel is not currently used for user-level personalization.
